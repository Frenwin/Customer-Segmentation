## Key observations

- `customer_id` is unique per **order** (99,441 = number of orders); the person is `customer_unique_id` (96,096 distinct).
- Orders span 2016-09 to 2018-10, but 2016 has only 329 orders with a collection gap and Sep/Oct 2018 only 20 orders: the analysis window is restricted to 2017-01-01 .. 2018-08-31 (20 complete months).
- 1,000,163 geolocation rows for ~19k zip prefixes (heavy duplication) -> aggregated to one median point per prefix.
- No customer demographics (gender / age), no registration date, no product cost, no discounts and no marketing data exist in the source. Consequences: acquisition date = first purchase, revenue (not profit) contribution only, KPIs of PRD 8.4 (engagement) are out of scope.
- Reviews: 814 duplicated review_id and 551 orders with >1 review -> latest answer kept.
- 610 products without category -> `uncategorized`.
