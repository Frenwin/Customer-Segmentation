# Marketing & CRM Recommendations

One play per segment, prioritised by revenue at stake. Every campaign should be launched as an experiment with a randomised hold-out (PRD 11: compare against a control group) because the dataset has no campaign history to size uplift.

| Priority | Segment | Customers | Revenue share | Play | Primary KPI |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| 1 | At Risk | 31,608 | 61.2% | Targeted win-back in the original category with a personalised incentive capped by expected value; prioritise the high-value-at-risk list (top-20 % spenders still inside the threshold); measure reactivation against a hold-out group. | reactivation rate |
| 2 | Hibernating | 41,746 | 18.6% | Low-cost reactivation (newsletter, seasonal campaigns such as Black Friday). | reactivation rate |
| 3 | New Customers | 16,961 | 14.6% | Onboarding journey and second-purchase incentive inside the 0-90 day window where most second purchases happen; ensure the first delivery is on time. | 6-month repeat rate |
| 4 | Loyal Customers | 2,789 | 5.6% | Membership / loyalty points on the 2nd-to-3rd order, category cross-sell based on their history, review-request after on-time delivery. | repeat purchase rate |

## Cross-segment actions

1. **Second-purchase programme** for every one-time buyer still inside the inactivity window (58,267 customers): personalised recommendation + time-boxed incentive between day 7 and day 90 after delivery.
2. **Delivery promise** — operational fix with the biggest satisfaction effect; track late-delivery share weekly next to review score.
3. **High-value at-risk list** (Power BI page 6): top-20 % spenders approaching the 278-day threshold — reach them before they cross it.
4. **Measure properly**: A/B tests with hold-out, success = 6-month repeat rate and incremental revenue; reactivation rate for lapsed segments.
